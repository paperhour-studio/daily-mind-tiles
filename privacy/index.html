Privacy policy

Daily Mind Tiles is a number puzzle for adults. Contact: paperhour.studio@gmail.com.

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Delete your data

Daily Mind Tiles keeps the puzzle on your device. There is no account. To delete that data:

  1. Open Daily Mind Tiles.
  2. Open Settings, then Reset progress. This deletes saved games, scores, and streaks immediately. Theme and other settings stay on the device.
  3. Uninstall Daily Mind Tiles to delete everything the app stored, including settings. That also happens immediately.

Daily Mind does not keep a server copy, so there is no extra retention period for games or settings. If Android backup is on, Google may keep a backup until you delete it from your Google Account.

Advertising data is different. When a rewarded ad or the consent form runs, Google may process an advertising identifier, IP address and coarse location, device and app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Daily Mind does not keep a copy of that data and cannot delete Google’s copy. You can delete the advertising identifier in your phone’s Settings by searching for “Delete advertising ID”. How long Google keeps it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

For help deleting data, email paperhour.studio@gmail.com.

Data stored on the device

The app saves games, scores, streaks, settings, and whether onboarding is finished. This stays on the device. There is no account and no Daily Mind server.

You can remove scores and saved games with Settings, then Reset progress. Uninstalling the app deletes the local data. If Android backup is on, Google may copy the app data as part of your device backup.

Ads

The puzzle works offline. A connection is used only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad for one hint or, when the board is stuck, to clear the two smallest tiles.

Those ads are requested through Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform. Google may process an advertising identifier, IP address and coarse location, device and app identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and your privacy choice where a consent form is required. The app does not sell this information and does not add its own analytics, crash reporting, or account system. Google’s use of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services.

Audience and permissions

The app is not directed at children. It declares Internet, network-state, and advertising-ID permissions because the ads SDK needs them. It does not read contacts, location, photos, or the microphone.
